    ESPConnect
    
            
    ESPConnect v1.1.23 is hosted as static files on this Hub. It can inspect supported ESP32 and ESP8266 boards over USB, including device and chip information, without loading the app from the internet.
    
            
    ESPConnect accesses the USB serial device directly from your browser. Use a Chromium-based browser, connect the board to this computer with a data-capable USB cable, then grant access when prompted. Close other serial tools first.
    
            
    
              Open ESPConnect v1.1.23 in a new window
              ESPConnect v1.1.23 release notes
            
    
            
    The board inspection runs locally in your browser. dBmap does not send ESPConnect your Hub access token or automatically register the detected board; linking device inspection to the registration form is a later step. Release notes and help links still require internet.
